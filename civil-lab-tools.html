MDD & OMC compaction calculator

Enter mould details once, then add the raw measurements for each compaction point.

1. Mould information

Wet soil mass = (Mould + compacted wet soil) − Empty mould mass
Wet density = Wet soil mass ÷ Mould volume
Dry density = Wet density ÷ (1 + moisture content ÷ 100)

2. Compaction test points

Use grams for all mass readings and cm³ for mould volume. Each row represents one moisture-content sample associated with one compacted mould specimen.

Enter mould details and a test point to begin.

4. Compaction curve

Dry density is plotted against moisture content. The smooth natural cubic spline passes through all measured points; the marked peak is used to determine MDD and OMC.

Engineering note: The reported MDD and OMC are determined from the maximum of a natural cubic spline interpolated through all five measured dry-density/moisture-content points. The peak is searched only within the measured moisture-content range. Use enough valid points to define the curve, including points on both sides of the peak. Confirm mould calibration, compaction procedure, moisture determination, units and reporting requirements against the project specification and applicable test standard. This tool does not determine acceptance or replace engineering review.
