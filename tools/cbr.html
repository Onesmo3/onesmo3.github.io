Soil Testing

California Bearing Ratio — CBR

Record the three CBR test points, soaked moisture and swell, then enter the penetration readings. CBR at 2.5 mm and 5.0 mm, the logarithmic trend and CBR at 95% MDD update as you type.

2. Compaction targets and equipment

Water to add for OMC = (OMC − NMC) × initial sample mass ÷ (100 + NMC).

The three CBR moulds normally use 2305 cm³. Use the target MDD, OMC and NMC determined from the compaction sheet for the same material.

3. CBR test points

Enter masses in grams. Pre-compaction moisture is used for the dry density; the after-soaking moisture is reported separately. Use one row per test (A, B and C).

4. Penetration readings

Enter the dial gauge reading at each penetration. Force = dial reading × proving ring factor. CBR at 2.5 mm = force ÷ 13.2 × 100; CBR at 5.0 mm = force ÷ 20 × 100.

Engineering note: CBR is calculated at 2.5 mm and 5.0 mm from the proving-ring force. The 95% MDD value is read from a logarithmic trend fitted to the measured CBR points, not from a single mould. If the 5.0 mm CBR is higher than the 2.5 mm value, check the test and the project specification before reporting. Confirm the compaction method, soaking period, proving-ring calibration, moisture determination and reporting requirements against BS 1377 or the project specification.
