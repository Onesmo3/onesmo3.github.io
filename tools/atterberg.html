Atterberg Limits — Liquid & Plastic Limit

Enter the four cone-penetration determinations and the two plastic-limit determinations. The liquid limit is read from the linear penetration–moisture relationship at 20 mm; plasticity index, linear shrinkage and shrinkage product update as you type.

2. Liquid limit — cone penetration

Enter four determinations. Moisture content = (wet container − dry container) ÷ (dry container − container) × 100. The chart fits penetration against moisture content and reads the liquid limit at 20 mm.

4. Sample preparation, linear shrinkage and shrinkage product

Linear shrinkage = (initial length − oven-dried length) ÷ initial length × 100. Shrinkage product = linear shrinkage × percentage passing 0.425 mm. The Excel sheet takes the 0.075 mm value from the grading sheet for its unlabelled check total; enter it here if you want that check shown.
