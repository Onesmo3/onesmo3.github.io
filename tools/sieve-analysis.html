Soil Testing

Sieve Analysis & Grading Curve

Record the sample information, enter the masses on each sieve and read percentage retained, cumulative percentage passing, grading modulus (GM) and grading coefficient (GC). Form A follows the riffled soil sheet (m1, m2, m3, m4).

Specification limits (% passing)

Leave a box empty if there is no lower or upper limit for that sieve. Sieves with limits are the ones plotted on the grading curve by default. Limits are saved in this browser for each form.

4. Mass retained on each sieve

Enter 0 for sieves where nothing is retained. A blank sieve is counted as 0 g.

Enter the initial dry mass m1 to begin.

5. Results

Grading Modulus (GM)
—
(300 − %<2 − %<0.425 − %<0.075) ÷ 100
Grading Coefficient (GC)
—
((%<28 − %<0.425) × %<5) ÷ 100
Specification limits
—
No limits set.
% retained = corrected mass ÷ m1 × 100
Cumulative % passing = 100 − cumulative % retained
%<d means the cumulative % passing the sieve of size d (mm)

6. Grading curve

Cumulative % passing is plotted against sieve size on a logarithmic scale. The blue dashed lines are the specification limits. A red point is outside the limits.

Engineering note: This tool performs the arithmetic only. Pass or fail is judged on the cumulative % passing shown (2 decimals) against the limits entered above. On Form A, m2 is the material passing the coarsest fine-sieve boundary (20 mm), m3 is the riffled portion of m2, and masses from 14 mm down are multiplied by m2 ÷ m3. Confirm the limits, the allowed mass loss, the sieve set, the washing and drying procedure, and the reporting requirements against the project specification and the applicable test standard. It does not replace engineering review.
