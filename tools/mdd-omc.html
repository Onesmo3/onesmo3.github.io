Soil Testing

Proctor Compaction — MDD & OMC

Enter the test information and mould details once, then add each compaction point. Results are read at the top of a smooth curve through all measured points.

1. Test information

2. Mould information

Wet soil mass = (Mould + compacted wet soil) − Empty mould mass
Wet density = Wet soil mass ÷ Mould volume
Dry density = Wet density ÷ (1 + moisture content ÷ 100)

3. Compaction test points

Use grams for all masses and cm³ for mould volume. Each point needs one compacted mould specimen and one moisture sample from it.

Enter mould details and a test point to begin.

5. Compaction curve

Dry density is plotted against moisture content. A smooth curve passes through every measured point (orange dots). MDD and OMC are read at the top of this curve (red dot), which can lie between points, and not simply at the highest measured dry density.
