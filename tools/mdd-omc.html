Soil Testing

MDD & OMC Compaction Calculator

Enter mould details once, then add the raw measurements for each compaction point.

1. Mould information

Wet soil mass = (Mould + compacted wet soil) − Empty mould mass
Wet density = Wet soil mass ÷ Mould volume
Dry density = Wet density ÷ (1 + moisture content ÷ 100)

2. Compaction test points

Use grams for all mass readings and cm³ for mould volume. Each row represents one moisture-content sample associated with one compacted mould specimen.

4. Compaction curve

Dry density is plotted against moisture content. A smooth curve passes through every measured point (orange dots). MDD and OMC are read at the top of this curve (red dot), which can lie between points, and not simply at the highest measured dry density.

Engineering note: The reported MDD and OMC are read at the top of a smooth curve (natural cubic spline) drawn through all measured dry-density/moisture-content points, not at the highest measured dry density. The top is searched only within the measured moisture-content range. Use enough valid points to define the curve, including points on both sides of the peak. Confirm mould calibration, compaction procedure, moisture determination, units and reporting requirements against the project specification and applicable test standard.
