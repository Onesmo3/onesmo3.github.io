Use + Add cube / − Remove in each age row (or × on a single cube) to change the number of cubes — 1 to 24 per set. Density = mass ÷ cube volume × 1000; corrected load = load × calibration coefficient; strength = corrected load ÷ cube factor (22.5 for a 150 mm cube).
